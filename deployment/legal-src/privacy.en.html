3. How data is processed (third-party processors)

  • AI providers. Recognition and narration are processed by AI providers on our instructions. OpenAI receives the photo you take to identify an artwork (only when our own matching cannot decide) and artwork names, and translates text. Anthropic reads photos of wall labels, and writes and checks the narration and Q&A from public reference material (which contains no personal data). Under their API data policies, neither provider uses this data to train its models.
  • Speech. Narration audio is produced by our own servers or by Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. Google receives only the narration text, which is public information about the artwork and contains no personal data.
  • Sign-in with Google is provided by Google, and Sign in with Apple by Apple. Crash reports and usage statistics are provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics): we use only its default automatic events (app open, screen view, session); we have no custom events in Firebase and have removed the advertising-ID permission; on iPhone we never ask for tracking permission, and the data is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone. We never see or store your payment method (card number and so on) — payment happens entirely inside Google Play or the App Store. We receive only a purchase receipt from Google or Apple, used to verify the purchase and grant the pass, and we store the order number, product, amount and time.
  • Photos you choose to send us are kept in private Cloudflare R2 storage, restricted to the European Union (the R2 EU jurisdiction); they are not bound to any public address and only we can access them, with a dedicated key.
  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Your rights (GDPR/CCPA)

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account. It takes effect immediately after you confirm and cannot be undone.
  • What happens after deletion:
    • Permanently deleted: your account profile (email, username) and the list of free audio works you unlocked.
    • The device's free quota is kept, unlinked: the device identifier and the free recognitions that device still has left are kept with the account link removed. If the same device signs in again, it continues with what was left and is not given a new allowance — otherwise anyone could delete and re-create an account to claim free quota again.
    • Your footprints and usage events are unlinked from you: the account identifier is removed from recognition events and usage events (and the device identifier from usage events), so these records no longer point to anyone; the statistics without the link (image fingerprints, recognized works, counts of screens and purchases) are kept for recognition accuracy and product statistics.
    • The text you wrote in feedback is deleted; the defect coordinates are kept, unlinked: the text you wrote in feedback is really deleted; what the feedback pointed at (which museum, artwork, language, type of problem) is kept with the account and device identifiers removed — that record says "this language of this artwork has a problem", and it has to stay for us to fix the content; deleting it would also erase an error that has not been fixed yet.
    • The photos you chose to send us are deleted: the photo itself, the note you wrote and the text you searched for are all deleted; only records that contain no image and point to no one are kept (recognition scores, the work you found).
    • Any pass you bought is voided immediately and cannot be recovered — not even by registering again; you would have to buy it again. A pass is a consumable product that is redeemed on the store's side (Google Play or the App Store) the moment it is granted, so "Restore purchases" will not bring it back. Before you delete, make sure your pass has been used up or is no longer needed.
    • Transaction records are kept: because of accounting and tax record-keeping obligations, purchase records (order number, product, amount, time) are not deleted, but they are unlinked from your identity and the personal data in them (the original purchase receipt) is removed. This is an exception permitted by a legal obligation, and it is not used for any other purpose.
  • Export your data: email appcraft008@gmail.com to request it. We will provide it in a machine-readable format within 30 days. It includes your account profile, free quota, footprints, the feedback you submitted, the photos you chose to send us (photos still within the retention period are provided as time-limited download links) and your transaction records.
  • You can also exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability and so on through the same email address; we will respond within 30 days.

5. Data security and where it is stored

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS; personal data such as accounts and transactions is stored on servers located in the European Union (France), and passwords are stored encrypted with bcrypt. Content assets such as artwork images and audio are kept in Cloudflare R2 object storage (they contain no personal data); the photos you choose to send us are kept in a separate private Cloudflare R2 storage, restricted to the European Union and not publicly accessible. We protect data with access control, rate limiting and daily backups.

6. Children's privacy

This app is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact appcraft008@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes materially, we will announce it in the app or on this page. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.

FAQ

Is GoMuseum free?

Your first 5 artwork scans and your first audio guide are free, no account required. A City Pass unlocks unlimited scans and audio across all four museums for the length of your visit.

Which languages does GoMuseum support?

GoMuseum currently supports English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Japanese, Korean, and both Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. You can start scanning as a guest — no sign-up required.

Does it recognize sculptures too?

Yes. GoMuseum recognizes paintings, sculptures and other objects across all four museums.

Will my photos be stored?

No. Photos are used only to recognize the artwork in front of you — we never store or upload your original images.

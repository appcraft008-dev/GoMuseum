```
GoMuseum turns your phone into an instant art guide for the Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, Musée de l'Orangerie and Petit Palais — four Paris museums, one app.

Point your camera at any painting, sculpture or object. GoMuseum scans and recognizes it in seconds, then narrates its story in a grounded, fact-checked audio guide — sourced, never invented. No searching a museum map, no scrolling a generic Wikipedia page, no fixed tour route.

SCAN & LISTEN
• Instant artwork recognition — point your camera, get an answer in seconds
• Narrated audio guides, grounded in verified sources, never invented
• Full text guides for thousands of works across all four museums
• Curated Q&A for many works — quick answers to common questions, right where you're standing
• Works in 10 languages, including English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Japanese, Korean, and both Simplified and Traditional Chinese

EXPLORE FREELY
• Search any museum's collection by artwork or artist name
• No account needed — start scanning as a guest
• Your first 5 artwork scans and your first audio guide are free

NO RENTAL, NO QUEUE
Skip the audio-guide counter and the deposit. GoMuseum lives on your phone and follows whatever room, or whatever painting, catches your eye — not a fixed route someone else picked.

PARIS CITY PASS
One pass unlocks unlimited scans and audio across the Louvre, Orsay, Orangerie and Petit Palais for the length of your visit.

PRIVACY FIRST
Photos are used only to recognize the artwork in front of you. We never store or upload your original images.

GoMuseum is an independent guide, not affiliated with or endorsed by any museum. Whether you have an hour before your train or a full day to wander, it meets you exactly where you're standing — scan the Louvre, scan Orsay, scan whatever's in front of you.
```
